## Caveats (load-bearing; do not oversell)
1. **Study bias is the named residual confound (declared in the prereg, §6).**
   Well-characterized genes accrue both more STRING edges (→ degree, matched) and
   more GWAS traits. Degree-matching absorbs much of this and PBS-matching is
   orthogonal, but it is not fully eliminable with these data. The honest tightening
   is a study-intensity covariate (per-gene PubMed count) as a future refinement.
   The result is "pleiotropy in excess of degree+PBS-matched chance" — degree-
   conditioned, not study-bias-proof.
2. **This validates the pile; it does not establish any single mechanism.** §3.2 is
   an aggregate falsifier: the *set* of candidate bridges recovers pleiotropy blind.
   Individual candidate genes remain HYPOTHESES (§12.7 — high prior density in an
   under-searched region), and interpreting any one by eye is still the forbidden
   §12.6 move.
3. **Still §12.4-bounded.** No dynamics; static allele frequencies. A passing §3.2
   test yields candidate mechanisms, never established ones.
4. **Contamination line held (§10.2):** the clean channel (GWAS pleiotropy, never
   in the derivation) is the primary; STRING/GTEx-derived annotations (which ARE
   the graph) were excluded as self-recovering. This is the non-circular validator.
