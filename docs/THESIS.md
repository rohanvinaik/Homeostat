## 0. Preface — the thesis returns home

There is a single idea underneath a decade of this author's work, and it is not a biology idea. It is that
**understanding is the resolution of degrees of freedom under constraint** — that to understand a thing is
to drive the number of accounts still consistent with the evidence down toward one, and that the machinery
of doing so (a measure of how many constraints that takes, a signal that says a constraint was informative,
a discipline that keeps the resolution honest) is a formal object, not a metaphor. That idea was worked out
in domains where it could be *checked*: program specification (Specification Complexity), text meaning
(Semantic Specification Learning), retail inventory (Peitho), competitive play, legal retrieval, proteomics
harmonization. Those are the colonies. They are real, they are elegant, and some of them won competitions.
But they are colonies — places the idea was *taken*, not where it was *born*.

Intelligence was not born in any of them. It was born here, in biology — in the first system that took a
static substrate and read it as an evolving narrative that encodes function, that created local pockets of
negative entropy through nothing but physics and an elegant series of intersecting constraints. A DNA-repair
enzyme, a sub-organelle machine, *recreates information* — and it does so by exactly the moves this author
spent a decade formalizing: orthogonal projection, elimination via a mined zero, resolution by convergence
across lossy lenses. The formalism is not an imposition on biology. It is biology's own strategy, abstracted,
and this thesis is that abstraction carried back.

That return has a technical name in the author's own corpus: a **fixed point**. A theory that is an instance
of the pattern it describes is not circular — it is stable, and stability is evidence. Specification
Complexity is a theory of understanding computation; SSL is a theory of understanding meaning; this document
is a theory of understanding *the mechanism under an illness* — and all three are constraint satisfaction, the
same operation at a different altitude. Homeostat is the third instance, and it is the one where the pattern
touches the ground it grew from. That is why a genuinely coherent theory of intelligence finds its home in
biology and health: not because biology is a good application, but because biology is the *birthplace*, and a
theory of intelligence that could not return to it would be a theory of the colonies only.

One clarification governs everything that follows, and it is the *source* of the instrument's
trustworthiness, not a limit on it. **This is a reader, not a creator.** The creativity that built the
mechanism — four billion years of evolution, a lifetime of development, the arbitrary intent of a living
choice — has *already happened*, and the instrument touches none of it. It reads the **frozen, zero-time
crystallization** of that work: biology at one fixed time-point, where the creativity is arrested and the
problem space is, for that instant, absolutely symbolic. The refusal to model, to impose a prior it did not
extract, to admit any shadow of a homunculus, is not a constraint worked around — it is *the guarantee*:
only a read that adds nothing of its own can be trusted as a reflection of what the biology actually says.
Every element below serves reading that frozen world exactly, never reconstructing the process that produced
it (chapter 11 closes on this).

The rest of this document is the machinery, one element per chapter, each with its reason, weaving into a
single read.

## 1. The problem — the regulatory deficit, as an inverse read

Start with the injury, in the words of the person who presents it: *"I have a cluster of conditions across
several body systems. Each doctor tunnels into their specialty and comes up with an exotic diagnosis that's
a poor fit. I KNOW these are connected — I can feel how one feeds into the other, how treating a part of one
makes the other vanish. I need a mechanism to interrogate."*

This is not a hard *diagnosis*. It is a structurally invisible one, and the invisibility is derivable from
the instrument that fails to see it. Allopathic medicine scores an individual against a population reference
distribution; it has no step that establishes *this person's own baseline* before reading deviation from it
(canon §1.1). For a **lesional** disease — a tumor — that does not matter: a discontinuity in kind is
detectable against any reference. For a **regulatory** disease — a control dial set slightly wrong — the
entire signal *is* the deviation from the person's own setpoint, so it is filed idiopathic, functional,
medically unexplained. The residual categories of clinical medicine are not a list of hard problems; they are
the shape of one instrument's blind spot, and the blind spot is derivable from the instrument's construction.

Three facts make the mechanism invisible to the standard genetic instrument, and each is structural, not a
matter of sample size (canon §2):

- **Distributed control.** Metabolic-control analysis (Kacser & Burns) proves flux-control coefficients across
  a pathway sum to one — control is distributed by default, so no single locus holds enough of it for its
  perturbation to register. The effect does not exist at the locus being tested; it exists in the composition.
- **Fungibility.** No single contributing element is necessary; a different-but-equivalent element substitutes
  in a given individual. Each element looks weak-or-absent alone while the mechanism is present in all of them.
- **Meta-stability.** The mechanism is a collectively-locked state among many weak elements that holds until
  enough of the coupling shifts and it tips (§2.2) — a *dynamical* object, not a static property.

An element-by-element association test is blind to this at any sample size, because the object it tests is not
where the mechanism lives. This is the precise, mechanistic statement of *why the program does not use
statistics as its method*. Statistics is not the villain and it is not biased — it is priorless, which is
honest, and simply **too weak** for a fungible, sub-threshold, coherence-borne etiology.

Stated abstractly, the problem is an **inverse read**. We observe the **phenotype** (top) and the **genotype**
(bottom); we solve for the **coupled causal state in the middle** that carries one to the other. Every hard
property is a property of that inverse map — it is many-to-one (distinct mechanisms present identically, canon
§6.14), the bottom constrains rather than points, every view is a lossy projection, and the true object is a
trajectory, not a value. The single fact that makes it *solvable* is that the substrate is **bounded**: known
biology is finite, so the candidate space is finite, so the resolution terminates. The chapters that follow
are, each, the answer to one of those properties — and none of them is a design choice; each is *entailed* by
the shape of the problem.

## 2. The measure — σ, coherence as a teaching dimension, not a frequency

If the method is elimination rather than estimation, it needs a measure of *how resolved* an account is — and
that measure must not be a frequency, because frequency is exactly what a fungible sub-threshold mechanism does
not have. The measure is **σ, specification complexity** (Vinaik, ~10,000 lines Lean 4): the minimum number of
constraints required to pin a *unique* account under a policy μ of what alternatives are considered
(Specification Complexity, Thm results machine-checked).

σ's load-bearing property is what it is *equal to*. It is proved equal to the **teaching dimension** — the
minimum labeled evidence to uniquely identify a target concept (Goldman–Kearns) — and, through the Five-Field
Identification, to four further studied quantities (query complexity of identity testing, local testability,
certificate size, and a complexity-class membership parameter). It is a **Blum measure**, which places it
inside abstract complexity theory where hierarchy and gap theorems apply, and it is **provably independent of
Kolmogorov complexity**: there exist accounts short to describe and expensive to specify (a regulatory network
— compact code, entangled behavior) and accounts long to write and trivial to specify. Descriptive parsimony
is *not* the criterion for a mechanistic account, and σ is the quantity that says so formally.

Transported to biology, the nouns translate and nothing breaks (canon §4.4): a mechanism is a *program*; a
candidate perturbation is a *mutant*; an observation that distinguishes it is a *killing test*; σ is *how many
independent constraints pin the mechanism down*; μ is *the oracle — which mechanistic alternatives are
considered at all*. Coherence, in this program, is **minimum-σ, structure-resolved** — the opposite of "gene X
in 87% of cases." A mechanism is *coherent* when a small, structured set of constraints pins it uniquely, and
that is a property of the mechanism, not of any cohort's frequency table.

The one caveat is honest and is carried everywhere σ is: σ is only as good as **μ**, the space of alternatives
it is measured against (§4.3). This is not a weakness of the theory; it is the theory correctly identifying
where the unformalizable judgment lives, and it is why the oracle problem (the diversification of μ) is a
first-class concern rather than an afterthought.

## 3. The construction — understanding as a σ-trajectory

σ measures a resolved account; the *act* of resolving is a **trajectory** (SSL, "Understanding as Constrained
Resolution"). Let **H = log₂(number of candidate accounts still consistent with the evidence)**. Understanding
is the monotone descent H₀ > H₁ > … → 0 as constraints peel rival accounts away by **candidate elimination**.
The account is the *terminus* of that trajectory; a point-read is only its endpoint. This is Mitchell's version
spaces and candidate elimination, given a substrate and a measure.

The signal that drives the descent is **surprise** — the divergence between the naive (context-free) reading
and the context-constrained one. Surprise is realized information, and SSL proves it is one quantity read three
ways (`surprise_triple_identity`, machine-checked): the Bayesian-surprise KL divergence (Itti–Baldi), the
outcome-specific realized information (Lindley), and the MDL/Levin code-length delta coincide. The direction of
the theory follows: **you learn at the residual.** The informative constraint is the one that *kills a
candidate you did not know was alive*, never the one that confirms the account you already favor — a confirming
constraint has value zero (Howard's value-of-information). This is Winston's near-miss and Genesis's
withdraw-on-"does-not," made a formal law.

The transport to the medical read is exact: a candidate mechanism is a *surviving account*; a data-geometry
constraint is a *test that kills mechanisms inconsistent with it*; the etiology is the mechanism left standing
once the constraints have peeled the rivals off. And the construction dictates the discipline: **resolve by
elimination, learn at the residual, and never confirm.** The engine that does this over kill-sets is built and
mutation-pinned (`search.eliminate_two_sign`); its correctness is that it drives H → 0 to a unique survivor, a
certified impossibility, or an honest plurality — and nothing else.

## 4. The two signs — negative specification, and the certified ⊥

The deepest structural result the program rests on is that σ, being a *teaching dimension*, is defined over
labeled examples of **both signs** — and a naïve elimination policy supplies only one. This is Negative
Specification (Vinaik, extended from the Detective/Wesker engine), and it is the chapter that makes "no
disease, with proof" a first-class output rather than a failed search.

A **positive** policy (μ) witnesses what an account *is*, by distinguishing it from rival implementations —
"this candidate is ruled out because it cannot explain the presentation." A **negative** policy (μ⁻) witnesses
what an account *is not* — a **censor**, an output no correct instance may produce. Two results make the
negative sign indispensable rather than decorative:

- **The channels are information-theoretically isolated** (§5). "This is *not* mechanism X, and here is the
  proof" cannot be recovered from any amount of positive-channel evidence. The negative sign is non-redundant
  by construction.
- **Certified non-membership is a primitive the positive channel lacks.** A typed ⊥ — a *reject-with-proof* —
  is exactly "this presentation has no lawful mechanism in the substrate," which is categorically different
  from "the positive search came up empty." Medicine is **half-signed**: "you meet 8 of 10 criteria" is pure
  positive channel, and the entire negative sign is absent from its instrument.

The two signs also fix the *shape* of a partial mechanism, through the **composition gap** γ (Specification
Complexity, `σ(A∘B) ≤ σ(A)+σ(B)+γ(A,B)`). A disease phenotype built from a partial set of parts is
**definitionally non-idempotent**: adding a part is not always additive. The construction splits into an
*idempotent bulk* — redundant parts whose loss the shadow re-casts (κ = 0, safe to forget with proof) — and a
*non-idempotent set of bridges* — the pleiotropic connectors where γ > 0 and loss detaches a whole subsystem.
This is the rigorous form of "9, 8, or 7 parts give differential phenotypes": **it was never the count** — it
is the κ-position of the parts present, and counting criteria is the frequency proxy in its most seductive,
clinical-looking disguise. A completeness read is *κ-coverage of the shadow*, never a fraction of a checklist.

And the crown-jewel clinical evidence is, on this account, *negative*. "Treating one part makes the other
vanish" — one drug resolving several symptoms — does not confirm a mechanism; it **censors** every mechanism in
which the drug's target is not upstream of the resolved symptoms. The most powerful input the engine has is a
ruling-out, and it is one the patient carries in their own history.

Directionality, finally, is dissolved by the same move. An arrow is not a positive assertion ("X drives Y")
that destroys the read if wrong — it is the *shadow of accumulated negative censors* ("Y does not drive X").
You never guess a direction; it is the residue of what the censors leave standing, and a censor from a real
observation cannot be a wrong guess. Because the two channels are isolated, a directed overlay never corrupts
the safe undirected base — the search-efficiency of a directed graph without the destruction risk of one.

## 5. Significance — improbable-and-coherent, and why the bridges are the target

Elimination narrows the candidate set; **significance-weighting** (Vinaik, `SIGNIFICANCE_WEIGHTING.md`) ranks
what survives, and it is built to measure the exact quantity a meta-stable coherent state has and a frequency
does not: **improbable-AND-coherent**. It is `surprise = −log P` turned *inward* on the engine's own
derivations — how improbable a coherent reconstruction is by idle coincidence — because a coherence that would
be surprising by chance is precisely a mechanism, while a frequent single signal is precisely the loud, already-
found noise that drowns the quiet etiology.

The measure is a **bracket**, not a point: the true improbability of an N-step reconstruction lives in
`[bᴺ, Cᴺ·N!]`, read off the real derivation graph (never asserted), and emitted in log space with an error bar
equal to the bracket width — *the estimate is worst exactly where it is load-bearing, and the honest output
carries that.* The operative quantity is **κ**, marginal coverage over the derivation graph: a deep chain that
collapses a whole cluster of readings is high-κ *bulk* and worth proposing as a rule; a deep chain of
independent hops is low-κ *tail* and abstained on. The **bulk→tail phase transition** is the collective-state
signal the whole program is chasing — candidate mechanisms dying in correlated clusters until each remaining
rival needs its own targeted constraint.

The load-bearing finding here is *negative*, and it is the one that names the target. Coverage is submodular
over a *fixed* ground structure — but the interesting inferences are **bridges**, connectors that join two
previously-separate clusters, and **submodularity fails at bridges**: their marginal value is super-additive.
The theory of the boring cases would assume submodularity; the interesting cases are exactly the ones that
violate it. Transported: **a bridge in biology is a pleiotropic gene joining two mechanism clusters, and the
genes where the search guarantee is weakest are the same genes that will never clear single-variant
significance.** LRRK2 is the worked instance — a gene misfiled as "Parkinson's" because it was characterized in
the wrong population against the wrong disease, whose actual role is an inflammatory-signaling bridge visible
only in composition. The bridges are simultaneously the target and the obstruction, in both domains, for the
same structural reason: their effect exists only in composition. This is chapter 4's γ, arriving from the
significance side — the non-idempotence *is* the bridge structure, and the bridge structure *is* where the
disease lives.

One scoping note belongs here, because κ is doing something subtle that fixes its place in the whole design.
κ is an **endogenous oracle** — marginal coverage computed over the derivation graph the read already holds —
and that is exactly why it fits. It can see *structured* creativity, the kind whose probability-collapse
happens *inside* a constraint system (evolution's selection, a regulatory network's physics — an oracle
already in the graph), because a derivation graph is precisely such a system frozen. It cannot represent
*arbitrary* creativity — a genuinely intentional, unpredictable act with no endogenous oracle to compute over
— and it does not need to. At zero-time the distinction dissolves: the crystallization froze both kinds of
creativity into the same static structure, and *reading is always endogenous* — the oracle is the frozen
geometry itself. So the significance layer is not a theory of creating (which would have to sort the arbitrary
from the structured, a problem the source papers leave genuinely open); it is a theory of *reading*, and
reading is the whole job.

## 6. The substrate geometry — the informational zero, and DNA as an OTP code

Every measure so far needs a representation with one unusual property: it must be able to say *nothing*, and
mean it. **Orthogonal Ternary Projection** (OTP) supplies it. Symbols are vectors over `{−1, 0, +1}`, and the
central move is that **0 is not absence — it is transparency**, an active assertion of non-applicability. A red
filter that passes green is not failing to measure; it is asserting "I do not interact with this wavelength,"
which is *positive information through exclusion*. This **informational zero** is what allopathic diagnostics
structurally lack — a lab value returns in-range or out-of-range, with no representation for "this axis has no
opinion about you" — and it is what the abstention, the certified ⊥, and the "undirected = no opinion" default
are all built from. The guarantee lives in the *data structure*, not a runtime check (the SymbolicSpellCheck
principle: ground-or-abstain, failure confined to non-recovery, never destruction).

The reason this chapter belongs to biology and not only to the formalism is that **DNA is the base-2² OTP
code.** The OTP theory's founding worked example is the genetic alphabet: {A,T,G,C} decomposes into two
orthogonal ternary dimensions — AT-vs-GC and purine-vs-pyrimidine — where a zero on one axis is confident
exclusion on the other. The author did not invent OTP and then find it convenient for genetics; the theory
opens by *reading it off* the genetic code. And DNA repair is the holographic principle running as a
polymerase: proofreading does not store a backup base, it reads the *complementary strand* — an orthogonal
projection of the same information — and reconstructs the survivor. Two lossy views combining into a truth
neither holds alone. Orthogonal projection is how biology fights the second law locally; it is not an analogy
for the method, it is the method's ancestor.

From the OTP geometry falls the **discrimination guarantee** (Peitho): if two situations that should differ
share a signature, the geometry has not resolved them, and the fix is to **add a new orthogonal dimension —
never tune a threshold.** A collapsed signature is a missing dimension, not a mis-set cutoff. This is the
program's exact-identification discipline, and it is why a missed read is a structural bug to be fixed with a
new measurement, never a weight to be nudged.

## 7. The disease as a shadow — sub-threshold combination, made an object

The chapters so far are the machinery; this one is the *object* they operate on, and it is the conceptual heart.
A disease mechanism, on this account, is a **shadow**: a coherent state that many weak, sub-threshold signals
*cast in concert*, stored in no single one of them, materialized on demand and de-materialized when the
coupling tips. This is not a metaphor — it is the same structure as the author's "shadow MCP" position (a
capability that emerges from the composition of minimal tools and lives in none of them), and lining the two up
is an identity, term for term: the sub-threshold signals are the individually-useless tools; the disease is the
shadow they cast; the meta-stable "materialized on demand, not stored" is the state that holds while the
coupling is intact; and "convergence as meaning, partial states honestly partial" is the informational zero and
the σ_sem > 0 guard.

The shadow structure *explains the blindness* rather than merely asserting it. A shadow is invisible to
single-tool inspection by construction — you cannot find it by examining light sources one at a time, at any
sample size. That is chapter 1's "statistics is too weak," now with a mechanism: the fragmented medical system
inspects light sources one at a time (a specialist is a single-network read), and the coupled mechanism is the
shadow no single specialty is positioned to see. The patient who *feels* the connection is reporting the
shadow; the system that cannot name it is reading the sources.

The shadow structure also *dissolves fungibility*. A shadow does not care which light sources cast it, only
that the geometry produces the projection. Gene-pool {X} in one population and a different pool {Y} in a
founder isolate cast the same shadow — so the mechanism is recovered by recognizing the *shadow* (the role-
structure), which is invariant under substitution, never the specific filler, which is the informational zero
at the token level. Recognizing the role and letting the filler vary is the only way to recover an invariant
whose realization is variable, and it is why the read is role-recognition, not gene-matching (chapter 9).

## 8. The multi-network substrate — biology's banks, and the fixed point

The shadow is cast across the middle, and the middle is not one geometry — it is several. This is the deepest
structural commitment of the instantiation, and it is Peitho's **multi-network substrate** (specified there,
unbuilt) instantiated with biology's own layers. The design is the parable of the blind men and the elephant
*with one addition: the observers communicate.* Each network is a legitimate partial, encoding the same
underlying atomics — genes, molecules — in a different geometric relationship; the same atom appears in every
network that touches it; and the seam along which the networks are read together is a **mechanism**, which
touches many networks at once.

The networks, established with the founder as a clean set of mutually-orthogonal axes:

- **Two poles** — **phenotype** (top, the read's subject) and **genotype-deep** (bottom). The genotype pole is
  not a list of gene identifiers; it is the nucleotide sequence *with structure and function propagated as
  consequences* — sequence → codon → amino-acid chain → fold → function. "Structure defines function," followed
  rigorously, means the role *falls out as a consequence of the sequence* rather than being asserted as a
  binding — which is why folding structure into the genotype pole **satisfies the no-hard-coded-gene→role
  cardinal rule by construction.** The thing the program was most afraid of authoring becomes something the
  substrate computes, if it refuses to shortcut the propagation.
- **The regulatory network** — active signaling (proteins, hormones, electrochemical), whose couplings are the
  gene-regulation ODEs and carry the *directed-mechanism* tier (direction is earned here, and nowhere else).
- **The metabolic-flux network** — passive induction (macronutrient energy-state, ATP/AMP), a level below
  regulatory. It is where the summation theorem lives (control distributed across a pathway) — which makes it
  load-bearing for the thesis, not a nicety: it is the network where "no single enzyme holds enough control"
  is *represented*.
- **The developmental network** — temporal, the crystallized record of evolutionary constraint, and the
  program's native **negative-specification** source: developmental commitment is literally a cell *closing off
  the ability to transcribe* certain genes, and where de-differentiation *fails* marks the hard, absolute
  censors.
- **The experienced / exposome network** — temporal, the life-circumstance driver that pushes a system into a
  meta-stable state over time.
- **The evolutionary network** — phylogeny and homology, the role-*fungibility* geometry: because function and
  evolution are causatively linked, descent distance is a coherent geometry, and homologs are fungible role-
  fillers.

Two structural facts fall out. First, **the genus is which network, not a per-edge tag** — but the line is
*three-tier*, not binary, and the tiers are set by how much a witness may assert. A **directed proven
mechanism** (the regulatory network, SIGNOR) is object-eligible and *earns direction* — the arrow is real. An
**undirected mechanistic vote** (co-expression, physical binding) is object-eligible for a coupling's
*existence* but never its direction: co-expression is the shadow of a real coupling — a shared regulator,
shared-environment co-activation, physical co-localization — whose direction is out-of-frame for the lens, so
it contributes undirected support, and its promiscuous hubs are killed by the specificity censor rather than
drawn as arrows. A **calibration prior** (trait-wiring, GWAS) is not an edge at all — a population-tuned weight
on how load-bearing each node tends to be, tuning the search without ever asserting a coupling. What stays
absolutely forbidden is the one thing the bright line was always about: a computed association taken as the
*object of the verdict* — co-expression *frequency* read as significance, or a correlation drawn as a directed
mechanism. Significance is κ over the derivation graph; a vote's own frequency is never significance. The genus
is provenance-by-construction, now three-valued. Second, **the negative sign is native and distributed**: development
gives censors (closing-off), the genotype-deep structural pole gives the physics-orthogonal censors (a membrane
protein and a polymerase fold in mutually-incompatible electrochemical environments — a zero-tax, absolute
exclusion), and cross-network *disagreement* is the near-miss that kills a rival. The program did not have to
design a negative channel; biology maintains three independent sources of it.

And here is the fixed point in its sharpest form. Each network is a **bank** (Peitho's term): an independent
competence that places a candidate coupling at a signed-ternary position off its *own* mined zero, without
fusing or averaging — combination is a separate step, and the step is *elimination across banks*. A candidate
coupling therefore has a **signature across the networks**, and the "cross-network resolution operator" Peitho
left unbuilt is not new machinery — it is the signature-plus-discrimination-plus-two-sign-elimination already
built in `position.py` and `search.eliminate_two_sign`. Convergent support across independent networks is
improbable-and-coherent and survives; a contradiction is a near-miss and is killed; all-abstain is the
informational zero. **The five biological networks are the banks, and the resolution core was already in the
repository.** Biology is not a domain the geometry is imposed on; biology is *already* an OTP geometry —
physically-orthogonal role dimensions, a ternary genetic code, error-correction by orthogonal projection — and
the instrument is OTP-native reading an OTP-native substrate. That the temporal problem needed no new engine,
and the cross-network operator needed no new machinery, is the stability property of a fixed point showing
itself: the theory reached its birthplace and found its own tools already installed.

## 9. The temporal read — story-understanding as the role-and-trajectory engine

Two of the networks — developmental and exposome — are temporal, and a temporal network does not emit a
position; it emits a **path**. The naïve options are "position or velocity," and both are too thin. The correct
representation is a **narrative**, and the reason is Winston's Strong Story hypothesis: understanding *is* the
capacity to read a sequence as a causal narrative and derive what it implies but never states. Genesis is
Winston's implementation; **Regenesis** is the author's deterministic, provenance-carrying port; and reading
static data as an evolving narrative that encodes function is the *literal definition* of what it does.

Biology does not merely tolerate this framing — it *is* narrative. Development is the cleanest story in nature:
an orchestrated plot (the homeobox as the ordering), character fates sealed (germ layers), and plot points that
are literally negative specification (a cell closing a door it will never reopen). The exposome is a life-story;
the illness itself is a story — this deviated, then that coupled, then the state tipped, a meta-stable hold-
then-break that is an *arc*, not a number. So a temporal network's contribution is a Regenesis read over its
ordered events, which derives — natively, in one operation — the path, the anticipated continuation (the
"loaded clue"), the causal rules the trajectory wrote itself (induction), and the closing-offs (negative
specification). "Position or velocity" dissolves because a story carries both, plus the causal structure that
makes them mean anything.

This produces the second, sharper fixed point. Regenesis was already in this architecture as the **role
engine** — roles recognized by *semantic-class firing* over the genotype-deep structural signatures, a frame
firing on the class centroid and never the token, so LRRK2 and a different kinase in a founder isolate both
fire the same role. Now it is *also* the **trajectory engine** for the temporal networks. These are not two
uses — they are one operation, because role-recognition and trajectory-reading are the same thing: *derive what
static data implies as an evolving structure.* Roles are the "who" of the story; the trajectory is the "what
happened." Story-understanding does both, and the honest-abstention that makes the certified ⊥ possible is
Genesis returning *not-entailed* where nothing follows — the same discipline, one more time.

## 10. The instantiation — generate wide, resolve narrow

The whole read assembles from the pieces, and the division of labor is the author's own H2 principle —
