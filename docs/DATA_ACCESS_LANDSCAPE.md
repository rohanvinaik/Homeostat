# Cohort-data access landscape (assessed 2026-08-28)

The four §13/Phase-2 results converged on one binding constraint: cohort-scale,
state-resolved data (§12.4). This is what that data actually costs to reach.

## The ideal — Genes & Health individual-level data — is GATED
Source: genesandhealth.org/researchers/apply-for-access (verified 2026-08-28).
- **Institutional affiliation is effectively required:** "the researcher(s)
  affiliated institution(s) is(are) required to enter into a data access
  agreement (DAA)." A solo unaffiliated individual cannot sign a DAA.
- **Fees are real:** £5,000+VAT set-up (£2,500 repeat) + £1,000+VAT per user/yr
  — ~£6k first year. This falsifies the checkpoint's "no funding" premise at the
  cohort step.
- Analysis runs INSIDE their secure TRE; mandatory NHS Information Governance
  training. Review is "light touch," monthly Executive Committee, all research
  areas welcomed (priority areas: diabetes/CVD/mental health/cancer).
- **Recall studies (new samples) are CLOSED to academic-led applications** as of
  Dec 2025 — so no new SPM/efferocytosis sampling via G&H right now.

## NCT04698291 — the single most relevant study (§13.5) — RECRUITING, results ~2027
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov API (verified 2026-08-28).
- ~200 Bangladeshi/Pakistani-origin volunteers with loss-of-function mutations
  in SPM-related genes vs matched controls, inside Genes & Health.
- Measures EXACTLY the resolution axis: SPM lipid-mediator profiling (LC-MS/MS),
  phagocytosis/efferocytosis assays, T-cell cytokines, genetic association.
- Sponsor Queen Mary; Study Director **Prof Jesmond Dalli** (major SPM/resolution
  biologist); PI Esteban Gomez Cifuentes.
- Start 2021; primary completion **Oct 2027**; **no results posted yet.**
- Read: this is the program's own thesis being tested by a funded lab. Can't get
  its data now (recall-closed, embargoed). Plays: track for 2027 results, or
  collaborate with the Dalli group — a collaboration solves affiliation AND data
  at once.

## Open, solo-accessible NOW (no affiliation, no fee)
- **Genes & Health open GWAS summary statistics** — "Feb 2020 datafreeze" +
  a data-downloads portal exist (search-confirmed; exact trait list not
  renderable via WebFetch — confirm in a browser). Summary-level from the EXACT
  target cohort.
- **44,028 British South Asian exomes**, high-autozygosity (Nature Genetics
  2026) — the founder-effect power §8.2 leans on, now published.
- OpenGWAS / GWAS Catalog / GWAS Central summary statistics (already used).
- GenomeAsia 100K / IndiGen reference panels (GenomeAsia site was unreachable at
  assessment time; re-check).

## Strategic read
1. **The purely-solo program caps at summary-level analysis.** That is real and
   publishable (method + hypothesis generation — what the four phases
   established), but it cannot cross §12.4's dynamics constraint alone.
2. **Two levers open the individual-level door, and both are the co-author
   thread already on the table:**
   - the physician collaborator's **institutional affiliation** → the DAA route;
   - a **collaboration with the Dalli/Queen Mary group** → affiliation + the
     exact-right data + the funded instantiation of this thesis, in one.
3. **The additive solo step that needs neither:** pull the G&H open summary stats
   + the 44k-exome results and redo the E/I/R / PBS / selection analyses on a
   REAL South Asian cohort's allele frequencies. This directly replaces the n=1
   prior whose weakness sank §13.3/13.4 — a cleaner test of the thesis than
   anything Phase-1/2 ran, obtainable now, free.

## Bottom line
The binding constraint is now priced, not just named. Full resolution needs
affiliation (→ co-author or Dalli collaboration); a meaningful, honest step is
available solo today (cohort summary stats replacing n=1). Nothing here is
blocked on more method — it is blocked on data access, and the access map has a
free near-term rung and two affiliation-shaped far rungs.
