## Part I — The Case (the actual conception)

**The clinical claim (canon §1).** Allopathic medicine scores you against a
*population average*; it has no step that establishes *your own* baseline first. For
**regulatory** disease — a dial set slightly wrong — the entire signal IS the deviation
from your baseline, so these conditions are structurally invisible and get filed as
idiopathic / functional / medically-unexplained. (For *lesional* disease — a tumor —
the baseline does not matter; a discontinuity in kind is detectable against anything.)

**Why standard genetics cannot see it (canon §2).** The phenotype is produced by a
**combination of sub-threshold signals**. Control over any regulatory/flux pathway is
distributed (metabolic-control summation theorem); most heritability is in the long
sub-threshold tail (omnigenic). So the effect **does not exist at any single locus —
it exists only in the composition.** Single-variant statistics *must* fail here, not
for want of power, but because it is looking in the wrong place.

**The real problem, stated plainly (the founder's framing — the heart of this doc).**
It is not that we lack the obvious signals. For ADHD the dopamine-synthesis and uptake
genes light up every statistic; everyone has already found them. The problem is that
those **loud signals DROWN OUT the other etiologies** — the ones that only matter in
combination, the ones shared with autism (AuDHD), the ones that never clear a frequency
threshold alone. The goal is to **stop letting the loud statistical signal become the
noise that hides the quiet, coherent etiologies.**

**The method is data geometry, NOT statistics (canon §3, §5).** Do not ask "does gene X
associate with disease Y" (a frequency question a statistic answers). Instead: treat
the data as a **geometric object** and find the **local entropy minima / points of
information-theoretic coherence** — the combinations of sub-threshold elements that
*hang together* — and read that **coherence itself as the evidence of mechanism.**
Improbable-AND-coherent, not frequent (canon §5.1: "surprise = −log P turned *inward*
on the engine's own derivations"). The coherence is the signal. A found mechanism is
validated by the falsifier (§3.2): **recover known gene function from structure that
never used it.** If known biology falls out of the recovered coherence, it is probably
real.

**Diagnosis is a story, not ground truth (Winston).** The allopathic "diagnosis" is a
post-hoc, motivated meaning-imposing stage — a *story* laid over the data — but it is
treated as a necessary prior. It is not the point; it is one narrative. Leaving meaning
to a brittle set of mechanical rules (ICD categories, reference ranges) is what fails
regulatory disease. The engine for meaning-as-story is Regenesis (Winston's Genesis,
ported; `mcp__Regenesis__*`).

**Ayurveda is a decorrelated carving-source, never an authority (canon §6).** Not
"traditional good, allopathic bad." A systematic tradition is a **battle-tested
empirical system that has been groping at these combinatorial mechanisms by
construction** — centuries of raw outcome-feedback, precisely because it never had the
luxury of the loud single-gene story. Its *explanations* are wrong ("heat," "spirits,"
"energy") and that does not matter, because its *carvings* — how it groups what combines
with what — are a prior on combination that is **causally independent of the statistical-
diagnostic frame** that produced the partitions under test. Unani (Galenic → allopathic
ancestor) enters at zero weight, as the negative control. Convergence across lineages
that never touched is signal; convergence within a lineage is shared descent.

**The formal substrate (canon §4–§5; the founder's own machinery).**
- **σ (specification complexity)** — how many independent constraints pin a mechanism
  down; a surviving mutant is an unconstrained degree of freedom in the account.
  Parameterized by μ, the oracle (which alternatives are considered) — where the
  unformalizable judgment lives.
- **κ (significance / coherence)** — marginal coverage / hub-score / the induction
  prior; deep coherent chains ranked by improbability-given-branching, carried as an
  explicit widening bracket, never a point estimate. **This is the coherence measure,
  and it is data-geometry, not statistics.**
- **The Informational Zero (§5.4, and OTP)** — abstention as a load-bearing third state;
  the empty banks ARE the signal; elimination via the zero turns O(nᵏ) enumeration into
  O(k·log n) guided descent on a potential field. The representation allopathic
  diagnostics structurally lack ("this axis has no opinion about this individual").

## Part IV — The Laws (mirror of the auto-loaded CLAUDE.md)

1. **Data-geometry, not statistics.** The method is coherence of sub-threshold
   combinations in the geometry of the data. A statistic is at most a cheap prior —
   never the method, the significance, or the object. (The load-bearing law.)
2. **Significance is coherence** (κ / entropy-minima / improbable-AND-coherent), not a
   threshold on frequency / association / enrichment / differentiation / hub-score.
   Stop at κ → 0.
3. **The object contains the data-as-geometry and the phenotype** — not a ranked
   statistic over the generic interactome. Bounded by §12.4 (no dynamics → hypotheses).
4. **Diagnosis is a story; the population/E-I-R signal is a search-order prior**; the
   traditions are decorrelated combination priors; none is the method or an authority.
5. **Recover coupling with annotation held out, by coherence not topology.** The §3.2
   falsifier and the LRRK2 blind-recovery control govern; abstention (the Informational
   Zero) is load-bearing; confirmation independent of derivation (§5.9).
6. **Iterate the geometry, not the statistic.** When a result plateaus, the first
   hypothesis is "the coherence object / the data geometry is wrong," never "add a
   better statistic."
7. **Measurement precedes proof; no carving asserted as complete** (canon §6.13, §14).

## Part V — The Pathology Record (this project's failures, kept recognizable)

**The single death, in four acts — statistics substituted for data geometry.** The
2026-08-30 session tried, exhaustively and honestly (all preregistered, all committed),
to make the population signal do mechanism-recovery work — and every attempt was a
*statistic*, on a generic network, with no coherence-of-combination and no phenotype
anywhere in the computation. The results are real and the discipline was clean; the
*conception* was wrong from the start, and these laws + the AGENTS.md gate exist so it
is not repeated.

- **Act 1 — the object was a statistic.** The E/I/R "PBS pile" (a population-
  differentiation *statistic*) was treated as *the candidate object* (the old CLAUDE.md
  LAW 2 literally said so). PBS is a search-order prior (§7), one input — not the object.
- **Act 2 — the "mechanism" was generic topology.** Variants were mapped to the
  **universal human interactome** (STRING physical ∪ GTEx co-expression) — a fixed map
  with no person, no phenotype, no regulatory state — and "bridges" were read as
  community-**participation** (a topology *statistic*). The gnomAD-vs-Pan-UKBB
  replication proved it: swap the entire South Asian cohort and 98.7% of participation
  values are identical while the PBS differs greatly — the passing gates were
  graph-topology, the population signal inert.
- **Act 3 — the validators were statistics.** §8.4 selection-**enrichment** (a
  frequency test) FAILED under honest LD correction (p 0.0005 → 0.985); §3.2
  annotation-recovery used **pleiotropy count** (a frequency proxy), and "passed" only
  because it, too, measured the generic graph.
- **Act 4 — making the statistic load-bearing broke everything.** Restricting the
  candidate set to the top of the PBS ranking made the population signal load-bearing
  (cohort seed overlap 0.997 → 0.318) but the gates then broke (0/8 configs replicated);
  feeding PBS softly as a κ prior *demoted* the known bridges. Across four
  operationalizations, there is **no configuration where a statistic does the work AND
  the analysis holds.**

**The diagnosis (why it kept happening).** The agent (me) reached for statistics
because statistics *feels* like rigor on a genomics problem — and the auto-loaded laws
themselves enshrined it. Every "harden the gate," "replicate the enrichment,"
"make PBS load-bearing" step was tuning *how to measure a statistic on a generic
network*, when the design calls for the coherence of a phenotype-specific combination
in the geometry of the actual data. The tell, next time: you are ranking things by a
score and calling the top "the mechanism"; there is no coherence-of-a-combination and
no phenotype anywhere in what you are building. Stop.

**What survives as honest, static-data deliverable (do not oversell):** a method-limits
result — a rigorous, multi-cohort, preregistered demonstration that statistics on static
allele frequencies over a generic network cannot recover regulatory mechanism (canon
§12.4 / §15 NAMED RISK, shown not asserted). The real next advance is the data-geometry
method (Part II) on the right object, and ultimately the dynamic / state-resolved data
§12.4 names as the binding constraint.
