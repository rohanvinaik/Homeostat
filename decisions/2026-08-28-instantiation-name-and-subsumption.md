# Decision: Instantiate as "Homeostat"; subsume Predecessor_Study; canon is the checkpoint

**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Status:** accepted
**Context:** The REGULATORY DEFICIT PROGRAM checkpoint reached ground-zero
specification and needed a proper project to fold in GenomeVault's genomics/biochem
machinery, the MentalAtlas ayurveda catalog work, and salvage from the earlier
Predecessor_Study.

## Decision

1. **Name: Homeostat** (Ashby's self-equilibrating device). Founder constraint: the
   name must sound like a name, must NOT be Ayurvedic/tradition-specific, and must not
   signal to a skeptical reader that traditional medicine is being used as a
   replacement or superior medical epistemology — it is an idea-generating surface for
   coherence and mechanism imputation. Homeostat states the thesis (per-individual
   regulation, cybernetic lineage) in mainstream control-theory language.
2. **Canon:** `docs/REGULATORY_DEFICIT_PROGRAM.md` is the verbatim founding document
   and governs over all derived docs. Its §15 status ledger controls what may be cited
   as proved vs. conjectured.
3. **Subsumption:** `~/Projects/Predecessor_Study` is subsumed. Its findings do
   NOT cross (derived under the annotation-first, single-variant method this program
   inverts); its plumbing may cross via `docs/SALVAGE_MANIFEST.md` by re-derivation
   only.
4. **Scaffold:** classical-AI gate installed before any architecture
   (`AGENTS.md` + `docs/THEORY_OF_THE_CASE.md`), mirroring the
   Kaggle_Killer/RSNA_Knee apparatus (theory doc with supremacy clause, sha256-pinned
   reference manifest, decision log).

## Alternatives Considered

- **Sanskrit names (Setu, Nidana, Charaka, Prakriti, Vaidya):** rejected — accurate to
  the ensemble ingredient but brand the project by exactly the surface feature the
  checkpoint's Warning 1 / §12.6 (classification-first reading) warns will get it
  dismissed.
- **Allostat:** close runner-up (allostasis = individual predictive setpoints);
  Homeostat preferred for the Ashby artifact-lineage and the tie to the founder's
  Soviet-cybernetics genealogy work.
- **Gephyra / Pons / Rheostat / Eirene:** name the method (bridges) or a demoted
  candidate carving (resolution axis) rather than the thesis.
- **Continue inside Predecessor_Study:** rejected — the old project's core
  analyses are annotation-first and single-variant; a clean instantiation with an
  explicit salvage gate is cheaper than excavating.

## Consequences

- All work in this repo is governed by THEORY_OF_THE_CASE Part IV laws, notably: LRRK2
  blind recovery gates novel claims; preregistered annotation-recovery lists;
  n=1/array data carries no analytic weight; κ → 0 stopping rule binding in advance.
- Public-facing framing must lead with control theory / statistical genetics; the
  oracle-ensemble ingredient is presented per checkpoint §12.7 (search strategy over
  partition space), never as a comparative-outcomes claim.
